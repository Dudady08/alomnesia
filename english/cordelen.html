Once Upon a Time... in Cordel

Year

2025

Duration

19:59 min

Synopsis

The singer-songwriter Elba Ramalho recites a poem that tells the story of cordel literature around the world, while we meet Manoel de Santa Maria, a cordel writer who, like many others, learned to read as a child by reciting verses to his illiterate father.
