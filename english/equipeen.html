Partners

Meet the partners who make the company work

Bruno Rafael

Founding Partner

Bruno Rafael is a filmmaker, editor, and colorist, co-founder of Alomnésia Filmes. He graduated in Cinema from the Federal Fluminense University (UFF) and began his journey at the age of 14, winning his first national award with *A Melodia do Comércio Popular* (2015). Since then, he has built a career of more than 10 years dedicated to audiovisual work, combining technical excellence and artistic sensitivity. He has directed and edited several award-winning short films, including *Once Upon a Time... in Cordel* (2025), winner of Best Film at the Caruaru Festival, Best Direction at the Miracine Festival, and Best Film of the Day (Audience Choice) at the Mangaratiba Festival, in addition to screenings at the Saci Showcase and selection at Incuba Filmes. At Alomnésia Filmes, he creates works that highlight Brazilian culture and social impact, such as the series *Família Fergus – Juntos Pelo Mundo* and *Era uma Vez...*, featuring artists like Elba Ramalho, Raimundo Fagner, Falcão, Zeca Baleiro, and Bráulio Tavares. He is currently finishing the documentary feature *Traços de Uma Nova Página*, about caricaturist Dan, with appearances by Zico and Buchecha. With a path that combines directing, editing, and color grading, Bruno turns narratives into striking visual experiences, establishing himself as one of the most promising young filmmakers of his generation.

Fernanda

Partner

A person with a disability (monocular) and recognized as gifted since childhood, Lúcia Fernanda grew up guided by love, equality, and respect, dedicating herself from an early age to social projects and volunteer work in communities, orphanages, and nursing homes. A polymath, she has been an athlete, award-winning visual artist, programmer, graphic designer, and self-taught restorer. At age 15, she founded her first company and, for over 20 years, left her mark in marketing, events, and branding. Today, she is a speaker, certified master coach, psychotherapist, naturopath, and a scholar of philosophy and ancient cultures. She works to bring education, health, and self-development to communities, villages, and quilombos, and currently applies her talents in design and audiovisual production to projects such as *Once Upon a Time... in Cordel*, *Traces of a New Page*, and *Família Fergus – Together Around the World*, among others, while also creating art, music, and literature in her free time.
